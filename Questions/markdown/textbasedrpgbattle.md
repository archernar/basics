## Java Exercises 
### Text-Based RPG Battle 

    Create a simplified turn-based battle between a player and a monster. Create a `GameCharacter` 
    class with `name` (String), `health` (int), and `attackPower` (int) 
    attributes, and a `takeDamage(int damage)` method. In your main method, create 
    two `GameCharacter` instances: a 'Hero' and a 'Goblin'. The battle proceeds in 
    turns: first the Hero attacks the Goblin, then the Goblin attacks the Hero. The 
    loop continues until one character's health drops to 0 or below. Print the status 
    after each attack and declare a winner at the end.
